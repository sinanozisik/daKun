Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how daKun Health (“the app”, “we”) handles information when you use the Android application. It is a single document: it includes the Turkish KVKK information notice (aydınlatma), the explicit consent (açık rıza) text, and a GDPR notice for a worldwide release. It is also written for Google Play User Data and Health apps policies. It is not medical advice.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller (KVKK veri sorumlusu) is Sinan Özışık, an individual developer. Postal address: Prof. Dr. Bülent Tarcan Cd., Meriç Konak 4 Ap., No: 17, D: 6, Gayrettepe Mh., 34349 Beşiktaş/Istanbul, Türkiye. Email: dakun.support@gmail.com.

daKun Health is a personal nutrition, activity, weight, and step tracking app. It is not a medical device. Meal analysis and calorie or macro figures are estimates, not diagnosis or treatment.

The app is intended for people 18 years of age or older.

2. Information we collect

What we collect depends on how you use the app.

2.1 Account and identity

  • Guest use: you can use the app without a daKun account. See section 2.5.
  • daKun account (Google Sign-In): name, email address, Google account identifier, and optional profile photo URL. Sign-in uses Google Credential Manager and creates a session with our cloud provider (Supabase Auth), not Firebase Authentication. The explicit consent in section 5 is given on this sign-in screen.

2.2 Profile, health, and fitness data you enter or sync

  • Profile: date of birth, sex, height, weight, body-fat percentage, activity level, calorie and weight goals, related goal history, and preferences (language, units, theme, reminders).
  • Nutrition logs: meals, dishes, names, amounts, and macronutrients (calories, protein, carbohydrate, fat, alcohol where used).
  • Activity, step, and weight logs (including optional body fat on weight entries).
  • Meal-analysis results returned by the AI providers (estimated dishes, amounts, and macros).
  • Health Connect (only if you enable it and grant each permission): we may read and/or write weight, body fat, nutrition, exercise; and read steps, distance, and active calories, as described in the in-app Health Connect rationale.

These logs and results are stored on your device. They are uploaded to our cloud only if you use Pro cloud sync (see section 7).

2.3 Meal photos and AI analysis

If you choose camera, gallery, or the system photo picker, the meal image (and any text you add) is sent from our server to the AI providers in the listed categories (see section 6) so a model can estimate food and macros. We do not store the photo ourselves (it is not kept as a field on meal or dish records in the on-device database, and our gateway logs never include image bytes). We do not send the raw meal photo to Analytics.

Photo analysis assumes that images are of food or meals only. You should upload only food or meal photos. Do not upload other photos, such as people or faces, identity documents, medical reports, or other personal or third-party content. If you upload such a photo anyway, it is processed only for meal analysis, like any other photo, and is not stored by us. Providers may still retain the request for a limited time, as described in section 6. The same usage rule is in the Terms of Use.

Photos are therefore not “ephemeral” in the providers’ systems: those providers may retain the request for a limited time for security and abuse monitoring, as described in section 6. We do not use photos to train our own models, and the listed providers are not permitted to use your requests to train their models.

2.4 Technical, ads, billing, integrity, and logs

  • An installation ID (UUID for this app install, not your user id), used for sync, abuse prevention, and related backend calls.
  • Device IDs: that installation ID, the Android advertising ID, and the Firebase Analytics app-instance ID.
  • Play Integrity device/app attestation tokens on certain backend requests (meal analysis, sync, ads-related checks).
  • IP prefix for abuse prevention: we keep an IP prefix (/24 for IPv4, /56 for IPv6) together with request counts for at most 1 hour.
  • Our server logs may contain a user id and a short excerpt of meal text. They do not include image bytes. Supabase platform logs may retain IP addresses for a limited period according to that provider.
  • Diagnostics: Integrity tokens, AdMob SDK diagnostic data on the Free tier, and crash logs (see Firebase Crashlytics below).
  • Approximate location: The app does not request GPS or location permission. Google Analytics and AdMob derive coarse location from IP address.
  • App interactions: Firebase Analytics records product events (for example paywall and purchase funnel) and app lifecycle (sessions). AdMob records ad-related interactions on the Free tier.
  • Free tier ads: Google AdMob (banner and rewarded interstitial), advertising ID, and consent via Google UMP where required (for example EEA/UK). Pro subscribers do not see these ads. For rewarded ads the app sends your app user id to Google AdMob as server-side verification so a completed reward can be credited.
  • Subscriptions / purchase history: Google Play Billing product dakun_pro (monthly and yearly). We do not receive or store your card number. Play processes payment. Analytics may record in-app purchase events.
  • Firebase Analytics: product events (for example paywall and purchase funnel) and meal-analysis metadata (status, token or size metrics). We do not send the raw meal photo to Analytics.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (crash logs): in release builds, when the app crashes, Crashlytics (same Firebase project as Analytics) collects the crash stack trace, exception type and message, app version, and non-personal keys (flavor, build type, version code). The Crashlytics user identifier is a SHA-256 hash of the installation ID; we do not send your email or name. There is no in-app switch to turn this off, and ad consent (UMP) does not cover it. Debug builds do not send crash reports.
  • Local reminder scheduling (meal and weight). We do not use Firebase Cloud Messaging for those reminders.
  • Consent record (daKun accounts only): we store only a server timestamp of when you accepted the consent in section 5 (see section 10).
  • Quota information used to apply analysis limits to your account.

2.5 Guest use

Guest use is anonymous. There is no daKun account and no Google sign-in. Guest data stays on the device. If you run meal analysis as a guest, the request is sent from our server without user identifiers, as described in section 6. We do not keep a consent timestamp for guests. The explicit consent in section 5 applies only when you sign in to a daKun account.

This section describes where data go, as a matter of fact. It does not claim that standard contracts or other cross-border safeguards are already in place.

  • Photos: we do not store meal photos. See section 6 for provider retention.
  • Free backup: you can export or import a backup file you choose (share / open document). That file is under your control.
  • Network traffic to our backends and Google services uses HTTPS.

Guest and on-device data stay until you clear app data, uninstall, or we delete them after a valid deletion request. Crashlytics crash reports are kept in the Firebase console for about 90 days. Analytics and ads partners apply their own retention. Backup files you exported remain wherever you saved them.

Yine de böyle bir fotoğraf yüklerseniz, bu fotoğraf herhangi bir öğün fotoğrafı gibi yalnızca öğün analizi için işlenir ve tarafımızca saklanmaz. Fotoğrafları kendi modellerimizi eğitmek için kullanmayız; listelenen sağlayıcıların da isteklerinizi modellerini eğitmek için kullanmasına izin verilmez.
